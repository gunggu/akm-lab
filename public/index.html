01 / Contract briefing

Case analysis

Base your explanation on the lease terms and their accounting effects.
Assumptions. Contract begins 1 January 2027. Currency IDR. No incentives, variable payments, initial direct costs, taxes or transfer of ownership. Use straight-line depreciation and zero residual value at the end of the asset’s useful life. The finance-lease rate is also the market rate for this exercise. For an operating lease, the stated rate is the lessee's incremental borrowing rate; for a finance lease, it is the implicit rate. Residual values shown are expected unguaranteed amounts retained by the lessor, not lessee payments. For beginning-of-year payments, show the cash paid on 1 January separately from the liability and receivable remaining after payment.
